## 1. Executive Verdict (Decision Point 1)

**Decision: STRONG GO — with an upgraded flagship Gaussian squeezed-vacuum benchmark (`b > 1/sqrt(2)`, `r < 0`) and clean complementarity with our sibling projects.**

1. **Clean continuity with the submitted *Communications Physics* manuscript (`paper/main.tex`, `0a8dba2`):**
   - Directly solves the displaced on/off detection obstruction (`c = 1`) documented in `paper/quantum_optics_future.md` (former Open Question (v)), using an independent mean-photon-number cap $\bar n_{\max}$.
   - Generalizes **Corollary 4** (finite-dim binary POVM direct-sum vector) to infinite-dimensional restricted state sets (**Lemma 1**), **Propositions 3 & 6** (attainment vs. non-attainment) to optical classical sets (**Proposition 2.5**), **Proposition 4** (click/no-click bias breaking ideal bounds for all $\eta_d<1$) to realistic displaced on/off detectors ($\eta, \nu$), and **Corollary 1 & Eq. (17)** (`eq:Rdirect-induced`) to universal Shannon, Tsallis, and Rényi ($\alpha \le 1$ and $\alpha > 1$) classical lower bounds.
   - Reuses our exact rank-2 operator identity $\lambda_{\max}(\ket{a}\!\bra{a}+\ket{-a}\!\bra{-a}) = 1 + e^{-2a^2}$ (from `quantum_optics_future.md` and `code/verify_bounds.py` lines 642–649), which gives the exact analytic **quantum** `off_off` ceiling (attained by the low-energy even cat state $\ket{C_+(a)}$) and pairs with $\lambda_{\max}(I+\ket{a}\!\bra{a}-\ket{-a}\!\bra{-a})=1+\sqrt{1-e^{-4a^2}}$ for the quantum `off_on` branch.
   - Ties naturally to Marcelo's optical cat-state context (`Lewenstein2021`, *Nat. Phys.* 17, 1104) for the physics-oriented Introduction.

2. **Complementary boundary with `quantum-light-followups` (no cannibalization):**
   - `quantum-light-followups` covers two Master's-scale tracks still at Milestone 1: **Project A** (discrete-variable polarization qubits, PBS multi-photon Fock squashing, steering) and **Project B** (continuous-variable periodically binned homodyne quadratures, Zak/GKP blocks, binary non-Gaussianity witness $L_2 > 1.580$, EPR steering).

Physical setups, mathematics, and figures do not overlap.

3. **What must NOT be claimed as new vs. what IS genuinely new:**
   - **Do NOT claim as new:** (i) unconstrained two-LO displaced on/off detection of squeezed vacuum along the antisqueezed quadrature ($r > 0$, already solved completely in **Kovtoniuk *et al.* PRA 2024, Sec. IV, Eqs. 37–39 & Fig. 8** for $b=\sqrt{\eta}a \le 1/\sqrt{2}$); (ii) generic calibration-box robustness for on/off detectors (already in **Kovtoniuk–Bohmann–Semenov PRA 2026, Sec. VI, Eqs. 86, 90–91 & Fig. 7**); (iii) majorization in photon statistics in general (**Medranda & Luis JPA 2015**; **Van Herstraeten *et al.* Quantum 2023**); (iv) priority for energy-assisted nonclassicality/QNG tests in general (cf. **Fiurášek–Lachman–Filip NJP 2021**, undisplaced $q_0 \ge e^{-\eta\bar n_{\max}}$).
   - **DO claim as genuinely new, experimentally compelling, and referee-proof for JPB:**
     1. **Complete exact solution of the energy-constrained classical majorization envelope $\omega_{\mathcal C_{\bar n_{\max}}}$ across the entire parameter space ($a>0, \eta\in(0,1], \nu\ge 0, \bar n_{\max}\ge 0$):** Lifting the $2b^2\le 1$ restriction via exact 2D $\to$ 1D concave-envelope Lagrangian duality (`WEEK2_EXACT_CLASSICAL_DUAL.md`, Theorems 2.1–2.4). We prove exact concavity thresholds for all five support functions ($b^2 \le \frac{27}{8}$ for $E_0$ and `off_on`, $b^2 \le \frac{3+\sqrt{6}}{2}$ for `off_off`, globally concave for $E_1$ and `on_on`), show explicitly that individual outcome-resolved branches require two-point coherent mixtures above these thresholds (so naive single-coherent bounds are unsafe!), and prove analytically that for the full majorization envelope $\omega_{\mathcal C_{\bar n_{\max}}}$ the globally concave click branches strictly dominate every bitangent—yielding a **universal closed-form exact envelope** verified to $10^{-14}$ by independent $C^2$-certified dual certificates.
     2. **Flagship experimentally accessible Gaussian benchmark ($r < 0$, $b > 1/\sqrt{2}$) defeating all three baselines down to $\eta = 0.70$:** While squeezed vacuum displaced along the squeezed quadrature ($r<0$) *never* violates exact $S_2^{\mathcal C}$ inside $2b^2 \le 1$ (correcting an earlier Week 1 draft diagnosis at $a=0.70, r=-0.30$), crossing $b > 1/\sqrt{2}$ with the exact dual unlocks ordinary ~3.5–3.6 dB Gaussian squeezed vacuum ($r=-0.42, a=1.15, \langle\hat n\rangle=0.187, \bar n_{\max}=0.22$ at $\eta\in[0.85,1.00]$; $r=-0.40, a=1.35, \langle\hat n\rangle=0.169, \bar n_{\max}=0.194$ down to $\eta=0.70$). It **simultaneously evades all three baselines**—(i) Kovtoniuk (2024) unconstrained classicality (exact coherent mimic $\ket{\mathrm i y}$ exists unconditionally whenever $q_\pm \le \kappa e^{-\eta a^2}$), (ii) undisplaced $a=0$ + same energy cap, and (iii) single displaced setting $\pm a$ + same energy cap—while delivering a **10–20x larger majorization margin** ($W_2 = +0.1383$ at $\eta=1.00$, $+0.1107$ at $\eta=0.95$, $+0.1041$ at $\eta=0.70$), cutting Hoeffding shots from ~300,000 to **2,500–4,500 per setting**, surviving source loss ($T=0.90$), thermal noise ($n_{\rm th}=0.02$), and dark counts ($\nu=10^{-2}$), and demonstrating both Shannon violation ($\eta\ge 0.95$) and Shannon information loss ($\eta=0.90, 0.85$) on the same state! We also keep the non-Gaussian superposition $\frac{1}{2}\ket{0}-\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\ket{2}$ as a complementary $2b^2\le 1$ triple-baseline benchmark.
     3. **Universality across Schur-concave measures, attainment phase classification, and exact ideal quantum ceilings:** One exact envelope $\omega_{\mathcal C_{\bar n_{\max}}}$ simultaneously bounds Shannon, Tsallis, and Rényi (all $\alpha > 0$, including $\alpha > 1$ via Eq. 17 of `main.tex`), exhibits both **positive-energy attained** phases (e.g., $a=0.60, \eta=0.85, \bar n_{\max}=0.10, \nu=0.05$) and **strictly non-attained** phases (Proposition 2.5), and connects cleanly to exact ideal quantum `off_off` ($1+e^{-2a^2}$, even cat) and `off_on` ($1+\sqrt{1-e^{-4a^2}}$) ceilings.
     4. **Distribution-free finite-sample & asymmetric-calibration certification:** Explicit failure-probability guarantees (Hoeffding / exact Clopper–Pearson binomial + union bound over worst-case asymmetric calibration boxes $\Theta$ with $a_+\neq a_-, \eta_+\neq\eta_-, \nu_+\neq\nu_-$, energy bound $\bar n_U$, and post-selection-free Lorenz ordering), going beyond Kovtoniuk (2026)'s asymptotic 1-sigma error bars and separating pre-displacement source loss $T$ from post-displacement detector efficiency $\eta$ and dark counts $\nu$.

## 3. Referee-Proofing Checklist (Zero-Revision Strategy)

1. **Never claim majorization beats full convex-hull membership with the same data** — frame majorization's value honestly: explicit closed-form/certified bounds, universality across all Schur-concave/entropic measures simultaneously, and attainment/detector structure.
2. **Never claim energy advantage from $r > 0$ squeezed vacuum**, and credit Kovtoniuk (2024) Sec. IV explicitly.
3. **Never claim $r < 0$ squeezed vacuum works inside $2b^2 \le 1$** — emphasize instead that *lifting $2b^2 \le 1$ via our exact classical dual* is precisely what unlocks the $r < 0$ Gaussian squeezed-vacuum regime!
4. **Always preempt the *"why not just $a=0$ + energy cap?"* objection** by acknowledging odd cat has $q(0)=0$ and foregrounding our **Gaussian $r < 0$ squeezed-vacuum benchmark** (works down to $\eta = 0.70$, experimentally routine) alongside $\frac{1}{2}\ket{0}-\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\ket{2}$ ($2b^2\le 1$).
5. **Never claim calibration-box robustness itself as novel** — cite Kovtoniuk *et al.* (2026) directly and highlight distribution-free finite-sample bounds + asymmetric displaced-detector parameters ($T, \eta_\pm, \nu_\pm, a_\pm$).
6. **Never use finite-grid LPs or uncertified Fock cutoffs as witness thresholds** — every classical bound uses our analytic theorems + $C^2$ interval-enclosure certificate; every quantum claim uses either exact infinite-dimensional operator identities (`off_off`, `off_on`, 3-level dark state) or certified tail bounds.
7. **Symbol hygiene when citing `main.tex`:** Clearly state optical conventions $(\eta, \mu, \nu, \kappa)$ up front to prevent confusion with `main.tex`'s qubit POVM parameters.
