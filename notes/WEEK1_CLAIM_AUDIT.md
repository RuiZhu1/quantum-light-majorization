## 1. Executive Verdict (Week 1 Decision Point)

**Decision: PROCEED (GO), with sharp positioning and zero overlap with our other projects.**

1. **Clean continuity with the submitted *Communications Physics* manuscript (`paper/main.tex`, `0a8dba2`):**
   - Directly solves the displaced on/off detection obstruction (`c = 1`) documented in `paper/quantum_optics_future.md` (former Open Question (v)), using an independent mean-photon-number cap $\bar n_{\max}$.
   - Generalizes **Corollary 4** (finite-dim binary POVM direct-sum vector) to infinite-dimensional restricted state sets (**Lemma 1**), **Propositions 3 & 6** (non-attainment) to optical classical sets, **Proposition 4** (click/no-click bias breaking ideal bounds for all $\eta_d<1$) to realistic displaced on/off detectors ($\eta, \nu$), and **Corollary 1 & Eq. (17)** (`eq:Rdirect-induced`) to universal Shannon, Tsallis, and Rényi ($\alpha \le 1$ and $\alpha > 1$) classical lower bounds.
   - Reuses our exact rank-2 operator identity $\lambda_{\max}(\ket{a}\!\bra{a}+\ket{-a}\!\bra{-a}) = 1 + e^{-2a^2}$ (from `quantum_optics_future.md` and `code/verify_bounds.py` lines 642–649), which immediately gives the exact analytic **quantum** `off_off` ceiling (attained by the low-energy even cat state $\ket{C_+(a)}$) and pairs with $\lambda_{\max}(I+\ket{a}\!\bra{a}-\ket{-a}\!\bra{-a})=1+\sqrt{1-e^{-4a^2}}$ for the quantum `off_on` branch!
   - Ties naturally to Marcelo's optical cat-state context (`Lewenstein2021`, *Nat. Phys.* 17, 1104) for the physical-oriented Introduction.

2. **Zero cannibalization of `quantum-light-followups` (Nov 5–18):**
   - `quantum-light-followups` contains two Master's-thesis-scale projects still in Milestone 1: **Project A** (polarization qubits, PBS multi-photon Fock squashing, steering) and **Project B** (periodically binned homodyne quadratures, Zak/GKP blocks, binary non-Gaussianity witness $L_2 > 1.580$, EPR steering).
   - The JPB manuscript is strictly orthogonal in physical setup (**single-mode displaced on/off detection + nonclassicality**), while sharing the conceptual theme of energy-constrained infinite-dimensional bounds (cf. Project B's $c_E(r)$). Pausing `quantum-light-followups` for 6 weeks does not waste or duplicate any future work.

3. **What must NOT be claimed as new vs. what IS genuinely new:**
   - **Do NOT claim as new:** (i) unconstrained two-LO displaced on/off detection of squeezed vacuum along the antisqueezed quadrature (already solved completely in **Kovtoniuk *et al.* PRA 2024, Sec. IV, Eqs. 37–39 & Fig. 8** for $b=\sqrt{\eta}a \le 1/\sqrt{2}$); (ii) general calibration-box robustness for on/off detectors (already in **Kovtoniuk–Bohmann–Semenov PRA 2026, Sec. VI, Eqs. 86, 90–91 & Fig. 7**); (iii) majorization in photon statistics in general (**Medranda & Luis JPA 2015**; **Van Herstraeten *et al.* Quantum 2023**); (iv) priority for energy-assisted nonclassicality/QNG tests in general (cf. **Fiurášek–Lachman–Filip NJP 2021**, undisplaced $q_0 \ge e^{-\eta\bar n_{\max}}$).
   - **DO claim as genuinely new and defensible for JPB:**
     1. **Energy-constrained direct-sum majorization framework** for two-setting displaced on/off optical detection ($\mathcal C_{\bar n_{\max}} \subset \mathcal Q_{\bar n_{\max}}$), resolving the $c=1$ trivialization (including the 3-level dark state $\ket{\chi_a}$) via an exact classical Lagrangian-dual moment problem (Week 2) and exact ideal quantum `off_off`/`off_on` ceilings.
     2. **Triple-baseline operational separation:** We verified a concrete state $\ket{\psi} = \frac{1}{2}\ket{0} - \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\ket{2}$ ($\langle\hat n\rangle=1.50$, cap $\bar n_{\max}=1.55$, $a=0.44$) that, across $\eta\in[0.95,1.00]$, **evades all three simple baselines simultaneously** — (i) Kovtoniuk (2024)'s complete unconstrained two-setting hull, (ii) an undisplaced ($a=0$) on/off detector with the same energy cap, and (iii) either single displaced setting ($\pm a$) with the same energy cap — yet **is certified** by our majorization witness with a large margin ($W_2 = +0.0945$ at $\eta=1.00$, $+0.0541$ at $\eta=0.95$). Moreover, squeezed vacuum displaced along the **squeezed** quadrature ($r<0$) also evades all three baselines and directly motivates solving the energy-active $A_{01}$ dual in Week 2.
     3. **Universality across Schur-concave measures & optical non-attainment:** One classical envelope $\omega_{\mathcal C_{\bar n_{\max}}}$ simultaneously bounds Shannon, Tsallis, and Rényi ($\alpha \le 1$ and $\alpha > 1$ via Eq. 17 of `main.tex`) entropy sums, exposes Shannon information loss on squeezed vacuum, and yields an explicit optical non-attainment instance where no classical state attains $\omega_{\mathcal C_{\bar n_{\max}}}$ (gaps $0.1267$ in $L_2$ and $0.0951$ in $L_1$).
     4. **Distribution-free finite-sample & asymmetric-calibration certification:** Explicit failure-probability guarantees (Hoeffding / exact binomial + union bound over calibration box, energy bound $\bar n_U$, and post-selection-free Lorenz ordering), going beyond Kovtoniuk (2026)'s asymptotic 1-sigma error bars and explicitly separating source loss $T$, detector efficiency $\eta$, dark counts $\nu$, and asymmetric settings $a_+\neq a_-$.

## 3. Pitfalls to Avoid (Referee Checklist) & Notation Note

1. **Never claim majorization beats full convex-hull membership with the same data** (label-blindness makes $W_2$ strictly sufficient: e.g., odd cat has $L_2 - A_{11} = +0.2556$ vs. $W_2 = +0.0132$). Sell **explicit certified bounds, universality across all Schur-concave/entropic measures, and attainability/detector structure**.
2. **Never claim energy advantage from squeezed vacuum ($r>0$)**, and credit Kovtoniuk (2024) Sec. IV explicitly for it.
3. **Always preempt the *"why not just $a=0$ + energy cap?"* objection** by acknowledging odd cat has $q(0)=0$ and featuring the $\frac{1}{2}\ket{0}-\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\ket{2}$ (and Week 2/3 optimized $r<0$ / cat) separation.
4. **Never claim calibration-box robustness itself as novel** — cite Kovtoniuk *et al.* (2026) directly and emphasize distribution-free finite-sample bounds + displaced-detector asymmetry ($T, \eta, \nu, a_\pm$).
5. **Grid LPs give lower bounds only** — every classical witness threshold in Week 2 must come from an analytic or globally certified Lagrangian dual *upper bound*.
6. **Symbol hygiene when citing `main.tex`:** In `main.tex`, $(\eta,\mu,\nu,\kappa)$ denote qubit POVM sharpness, biases, and $\max(|\mu|+\eta,|\nu|+\zeta)$; in the JPB manuscript, $(\eta,\mu,\nu,\kappa)$ denote detector efficiency, classical measure, dark-count mean, and $e^{-\nu}$. State the optical conventions cleanly at the outset to avoid cross-paper confusion.
